Frequently asked questions

What is the best free syllabus tracker for JEE and NEET?

Sticky Notes by Prepex is a free browser-based tracker that covers the full JEE (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) and NEET (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) syllabus, letting you mark each chapter as studied or revised and see live progress bars per subject.

How does the Pomodoro timer help JEE/NEET preparation?

The Pomodoro technique breaks study time into focused sessions (default 25 minutes) followed by short breaks. This built-in timer lets you tag each focus session with a subject and chapter, log a daily focus history, and avoid burnout during long revision days.

How do I track how many questions I've solved for JEE Mains or NEET?

Use the Question Log tab to record every question you attempt with its subject, chapter, difficulty, and result (correct, incorrect, or skipped). The Dashboard then automatically calculates your accuracy overall and per subject.

Is Sticky Notes by Prepex free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no signup. All your data is stored locally in your browser. For a more complete, cloud-synced JEE/NEET preparation experience, check out Prepex.
